Phase-Space Plots of a Neural Network

These are two phase-space plots of the 500 hPa Self-Organized Map from the first three months of 2023 and 2026. The shaded numbers are the dates that each SOM node was observed. The 12z data for each day was used. There are some patterns that occurred in both years, but there are also patterns that are unique to each year. Of particular interest are clusters of patterns around (2, 20) and (3, 36) in 2023; and (21, 0) and (5, 9) in 2026. When looking at the Antecedent Precipitation Index for the winters of 2023 and 2026, in January of both years, precipitation was occurring across the western United States. A series of Atmospheric River (AR) episodes over California, Oregon and Washington resulted in API maxima over those regions. In particular, over the Olympic Mountains and Cascade Range of Washington and the Sierra Nevada Range in northern California. Generally moist conditions continued in February of 2023, but in early February 2026, dry conditions temporarily developed over northern California, before some improvement is seen at the end of the month. While March 2023 remained moist, in March of 2026, precipitation stops falling across the southwestern United States, and by mid-March the API has fallen to zero across the eniter southwestern quarter of the United States.
